Nomad Check Support

Nomad Check provides product and account support by email.

Product, account or subscription access

Email: support@getnomadcheck.com

Include:

  • Device model and operating-system version
  • Nomad Check app version
  • A short description of what happened
  • Steps that led to the issue
  • A screenshot if it does not reveal sensitive information

Do not send passwords, authentication links, passport copies, passport numbers, payment-card details or bank documents.

Privacy requests

Email: privacy@getnomadcheck.com

Use this address for access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests.

Legal notices

Email: legal@getnomadcheck.com

Report incorrect information or a broken official source

Email: support@getnomadcheck.com

Include the country, screen, statement, official source and date accessed where possible.

Nomad Check support cannot provide personal immigration, legal, tax, medical or insurance advice and cannot contact a government authority on your behalf.

Billing, cancellation and refunds

Open the Subscription, Cancellation and Refunds page and select the store used for purchase:

https://getnomadcheck.com/legal/subscription

The page displays only the relevant platform controls after selection.

Account deletion

Instructions are available at:

https://getnomadcheck.com/legal/account-deletion

Emergency

Nomad Check is not an emergency service. Contact the local emergency authority directly.
