Information Sources and Review Method

Last updated: 2026-10-06

This page explains how Nomad Check gathers, reviews and labels travel, immigration and related information.

Source Priority

Where available, Nomad Check prioritizes:

  1. Published laws and regulations
  2. Official immigration, foreign-affairs, tax, health and other government websites
  3. Embassies and consulates
  4. Official application portals, forms and instructions
  5. Reputable secondary material only when necessary to locate or explain a primary source

Third-party articles, search results and AI-generated text are not treated as authoritative legal sources.

Use of Automated Tools

Automated tools, including AI, may assist with:

  • Locating candidate official sources
  • Translating and summarizing source text
  • Extracting criteria and dates
  • Comparing changes between source versions
  • Structuring information for display

Automated output can omit context or produce errors. It does not replace the underlying official source.

Review Status Labels

Nomad Check uses the following labels:

LabelMeaning
Source checked [date]The cited source was accessed and checked for the displayed topic on that date
Needs reviewThe source or interpretation has not completed the current review process
Source unavailableThe cited source could not be opened or is no longer available
Based on your informationThe output depends on information entered or selected by the user
User-providedThe displayed date or limit was entered or confirmed by the user, not checked by Nomad Check
EstimateThe value is calculated and may differ from an authority's determination

Nomad Check does not use a stronger approval-style label unless a documented review process and the record for that exact topic support it.

Meaning of a Source-Check Date

A Source checked date:

  • Applies only to the cited topic and source
  • Does not establish that every relevant law, rule or exception was checked
  • Does not establish that the source has remained unchanged
  • Must come from a recorded source-check event, not a build date or general database timestamp

User-Provided Information

When Nomad Check displays Based on the stay limit you provided or the User-provided label, the date or limit came from the user. It is not converted into a source-checked fact merely because the app uses it in a calculation.

Update Approach

Nomad Check aims to review source material on a recurring basis, including automated monthly checks where available. This is an operating target, not a promise that every country or topic is reviewed each month.

Urgent changes can occur between scheduled reviews.

Professional Review

Unless expressly identified on a specific item, content is not reviewed or approved by a lawyer, licensed immigration professional, tax adviser or government authority.

Broken or Unavailable Sources

If a cited link cannot be opened, the app should display Source unavailable and provide a way to report the problem. A failed link does not convert the app's summary into an authoritative source.

Report an Error

If you find an outdated source, broken link or possible error, contact support@getnomadcheck.com and include:

  • Country and screen
  • The statement you believe is incorrect
  • The official source, if available
  • The date you accessed it

We may investigate reports, but we cannot provide personal legal advice or promise a response before a travel deadline.
