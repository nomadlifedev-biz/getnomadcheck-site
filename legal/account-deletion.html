Delete Your Nomad Check Account and Data

You can initiate deletion directly in the Nomad Check app.

Before You Delete

Deleting your Nomad Check account does not cancel a subscription purchased through an app store. To stop future renewal, manage the subscription through the store used for purchase.

If you subscribed through Apple

Use Apple's subscription-management controls before deleting the Nomad Check account:

https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428

If you subscribed through Google Play

Use Google Play's subscription-management controls before deleting the Nomad Check account:

https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

Delete in the App

  1. Open Nomad Check.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Login and Security or the final account-settings label.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review what will be deleted.
  6. Confirm deletion.

Providing a reason or written feedback is optional. You can continue without providing either.

What We Delete

Deletion removes the following from Nomad Check's active systems:

  • Nomad Check account and authentication record
  • Email and name stored with the account
  • Profile and eligibility information
  • Passport country and optional passport-expiration information
  • Work, employer, client, income-range and visa-status information
  • Current stays, planned trips and travel history
  • Cloud-synchronized settings and records associated with the account

The app also removes Nomad Check local data from the device used to complete deletion, including temporary corrupt-backup records covered by the deletion process.

Guest Accounts

Nomad Check's guest mode stores your data only on your device; no server account exists and nothing is uploaded until you create an account. Accounts created before 2026-09-26 under the previous guest design are removed in a one-time cleanup before launch.

Information That May Remain Temporarily

  • Provider infrastructure logs (authentication, database and request logs) for up to 1 day under the provider's current plan; the current hosting plan includes no provider-managed database backups
  • Limited subscription or transaction records where needed for billing, fraud prevention, accounting or legal duties
  • Optional deletion feedback for a maximum of 12 months, provided it is stored without email, user ID, plan ID or another direct account identifier
  • Resend delivery records for 30 days

The app stores separately control their transaction records under their own policies.

Deleting the app account stops account-linked processing but does not by itself cancel a store subscription.

If You Cannot Access the App

Email privacy@getnomadcheck.com from the address associated with your account and request Nomad Check account deletion. We process these requests within a reasonable time.

We may send an authentication link or request limited information to confirm account control; we verify the request through the account's own email address and do not ask you to supply new personal information. Do not send a password, passport copy, passport number, authentication link, payment-card information or bank document.

If you use the app without an account, your data exists only on your device; we hold nothing on our servers to identify or delete. (For legacy pseudonymous accounts created before 2026-09-26, the one-time cleanup described under Guest Accounts applies.)

Confirmation

We will confirm completion in the app or through the verified email address when available. Contact privacy@getnomadcheck.com if deletion fails.
