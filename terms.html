4. Accounts and data

On a fresh online start, the app automatically creates an anonymous Supabase account when it finds no existing session or recorded identity and local storage can be read. This process does not wait for a button tap or consent.

After initial setup, saved profile and travel information is uploaded automatically, including before you link an email or Google account. "Guest" does not mean that your information stays only on your device.

You can link an email or Google account. If you link Google while your profile nickname is empty, the app saves your Google display name as your nickname and uploads it with your profile.

Signing out leaves your profile and travel data on the device. Someone else using that device can see retained information. Local content is not separated by account, and a later guest edit may upload retained information under a guest identity.

For a linked account, the deletion entry is Settings → Login and Security → Delete my account. The app sends a server deletion request before local cleanup. If that request fails, local cleanup does not start. The cleanup includes diagnostic copies of unreadable data, but complete removal on Android and the deployed server's deletion scope have not been verified.

Guests who have never linked an account cannot access that deletion entry. You can submit a request at getnomadcheck.com/delete; that page explains what we can and cannot identify.

A restoration failure screen can prevent access to account controls. After a successful account-deletion flow, the identity process restarts and may create a new anonymous account when connected.

5. Saving, synchronization, and availability

Saving on your device and synchronization with the server are separate operations. Different parts of one change are uploaded separately. A change can reach the server only in part.

Synchronization of future plans and stay history can remove server records that are missing from the device's collection. It is not a guarantee that all records from different devices will be combined. Using the same account on multiple devices may overwrite or remove records.

Signing in to an account that already has data replaces the guest information on that device with the account's data. This cannot be undone.

Local saving can fail without an on-screen warning. Unsaved changes may be lost when the app closes. Restoration failures can also pause editing. We do not guarantee uninterrupted access, successful synchronization, or recovery of every record.
